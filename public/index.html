ERP Technical Implementation

Oracle NetSuite — end-to-end implementation delivery

What We Deliver

We provide full-cycle Oracle NetSuite implementation — from discovery and configuration through integration, testing, go-live, and post-launch support. Our team ensures your NetSuite environment is built to match your exact business processes, with every module, workflow, and customisation aligned before you go live.

Implementation Phases

  • System Configuration — set up NetSuite modules (Financials, Inventory, CRM, Procurement), configure custom fields, forms, roles, and permissions aligned to your business structure
  • SuiteScript & SuiteFlow Customisation — develop custom scripts and workflows to handle business logic that goes beyond NetSuite's native configuration capabilities
  • Integration — connect NetSuite with your existing platforms such as e-commerce, payroll, and third-party tools via REST API, or EDI
  • Testing — conduct unit testing, system integration testing, and guided user acceptance testing with your team to validate every process before go-live
  • Post-Launch Support — hypercare support, bug resolution, performance monitoring, and ongoing NetSuite optimisation after go-live

Platforms & Technologies

  • Oracle NetSuite (ERP, CRM)
  • SuiteCloud Development Framework (SDF)
  • SuiteScript 2.x, SuiteFlow
  • NetSuite REST API

Industries Served

We deliver NetSuite implementations across a range of industries, applying industry-specific module configurations and best practices to each engagement — with offshore delivery support from our Philippines and Japan teams.

API Integration

Connecting your systems seamlessly — from REST APIs to real-time event-driven integrations.

What We Deliver

We design, build, and maintain integrations that allow your business systems to communicate reliably in real time — eliminating data silos, reducing manual effort, and keeping your operations in sync across platforms.

1. API Integration (REST & Custom APIs)

We connect your platforms through modern REST APIs and custom-built integration layers, ensuring your data moves accurately and securely between systems.

  • NetSuite EDI Integration — map and sync EDI documents directly into NetSuite records such as Sales Orders, Vendor Bills, and Item Fulfillments
  • NetSuite REST API — read and write NetSuite records (Customers, Orders, Inventory, Financials) via REST
  • Custom RESTful APIs — design and build purpose-built APIs that expose your business logic to internal tools or third-party platforms
  • Authentication & Security — OAuth 2.0, Token-Based Auth, and API key management with HTTPS/TLS encryption on all endpoints

2. Webhook & Event-Based Integration

We implement event-driven architectures that allow NetSuite and your external systems to send and receive real-time data the moment something happens — no polling, no delays.

  • Outbound Webhooks from NetSuite — trigger instant notifications to external systems when records are created or updated (e.g. new Sales Order, fulfilled shipment, approved invoice)
  • Inbound Webhooks to NetSuite — receive real-time events from external platforms and automatically create or update NetSuite records
  • NetSuite SuiteScript Event Listeners — use User Event and Scheduled Scripts to react to record changes and push data to external endpoints in real time
  • End-to-End Event Logging — full audit trail of every event sent and received, with alerting for failed deliveries or unexpected payloads

Our Approach

We take an integration-first mindset — mapping your data flows before writing a single line of code, validating every field transformation, and building with error recovery in mind so your integrations stay reliable long after go-live.

Process Automation & Data Migration

Streamline your NetSuite workflows and move your data with confidence.

What We Deliver

We help businesses eliminate manual bottlenecks and transition data safely into NetSuite — ensuring your operations run efficiently from day one and your records are accurate, complete, and ready to use.

1. Process Automation

We identify repetitive, manual tasks inside and around NetSuite and replace them with reliable automated workflows — freeing your team to focus on higher-value work.

  • NetSuite Workflow Automation (SuiteFlow) — configure approval flows, status transitions, email alerts, and field updates that trigger automatically based on record changes or scheduled conditions
  • SuiteScript Automation — write custom User Event, Scheduled, and Map/Reduce scripts to handle complex business logic that goes beyond native workflow capabilities
  • Saved Search & Report Scheduling — automate the delivery of key reports and saved searches to stakeholders on a daily, weekly, or monthly basis
  • Transaction Auto-Generation — automatically create Sales Orders, Purchase Orders, Invoices, or Fulfillments based on triggers such as inventory thresholds, customer activity, or incoming EDI/API data

2. Data Migration

We plan and execute structured, validated data migrations into NetSuite — whether you are moving from a legacy ERP, spreadsheets, or another platform — with zero data loss and minimal business disruption.

  • Data Assessment & Mapping — analyse your source data, identify gaps and duplicates, and map each field to the correct NetSuite record type (Customers, Vendors, Items, Transactions, etc.)
  • Data Cleansing & Standardisation — normalise formats, resolve inconsistencies, and enrich records before import to ensure clean, reliable data in NetSuite from day one
  • NetSuite CSV Import — use NetSuite's native CSV Import tool to load records accurately while respecting validation rules and mandatory fields
  • Post-Migration Reconciliation — verify record counts, financial balances, and key fields after migration to confirm data integrity before decommissioning legacy systems

Tools & Technologies

  • NetSuite SuiteFlow, SuiteScript 2.x (User Event, Scheduled, Map/Reduce, RESTlet)
  • NetSuite CSV Import
  • Excel / Google Sheets for data preparation and mapping templates
